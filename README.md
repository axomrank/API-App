# Assam Assistant Professor Recruitment API Score Calculator (`API-App`)

A lightweight, front-end web utility for calculating candidate Academic Performance Indicator (API) scores and recruitment markings for Assistant Professor recruitment in Assam colleges and universities.

---

## 📋 Overview

In Assam collegiate and higher education recruitment, candidates for the post of Assistant Professor are evaluated based on a structured composite marking guideline comprising academic performance, research credentials, teaching experience, and special co-curricular achievements. 

This tool provides an instant, interactive calculator to tally composite marks accurately without manual spreadsheet computation.

---

## 🌟 Evaluation Criteria & Marking Structure

### 1. Academic Record
* **10th (HSLC) Percentage:** Base academic qualification score.
* **12th (HSSLC) Percentage:** Higher secondary performance weightage.
* **Bachelor's Degree Percentage:** Undergraduate degree score.
* **Master's Degree Percentage:** Postgraduate performance score.

### 2. Research Credentials & Publications
* **Ph.D. Degree:** Weightage for doctoral qualification.
* **M.Phil. Degree:** Weightage for pre-doctoral research.
* **UGC-Care Listed Publications:** Points for published research papers in approved journals.
* **Scopus-Indexed Publications:** International standard publication credit.
* **Book Chapters (with ISBN):** Credit for scholarly book contributions.

### 3. Teaching Experience & Special Merits
* **Teaching Experience (Years):** Approved service in accredited higher education institutions.
* **NCC 'C' Certificate:** Extra credit for NCC cadet distinction.
* **University Gold Medal / Youth Festival:** Honors recognition in academics or cultural events.
* **Sports Representation:** State representation for Assam at National or International sporting events.
